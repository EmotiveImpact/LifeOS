Questions, answered.

Who is Life for?

Adults in the UK who carry a lot in their heads: bills, health, family, work. Tax, benefit and money figures use UK rules.

Does Life give medical or financial advice?

No. Life gives wellness guidance and money guidance from published rules. For anything important, it points you to the NHS, HMRC or a free service such as MoneyHelper.

Is it on Android?

Life starts on iPhone, and Android follows. Join the waitlist and we’ll tell you when it’s ready.

What happens when the trial ends?

If you keep Life, it’s £7.99 a month or £79.99 a year, charged to your Apple ID. Cancel in your App Store settings before the trial ends and you won’t pay anything.

Can I take my data with me?

Yes. Download everything Life knows in one file, or delete it all, whenever you like.
