## 3. What Life is, and what it isn't

Life helps you notice what matters, plan your day and keep on top of your health, money and life admin. It gives you information and suggestions based on what you tell it, your own readings and published rules.

- **It is wellness guidance, not medical care.** Life is not a medical device. It doesn't diagnose, treat or prevent any illness, and its readiness, sleep, stress and food figures are estimates for everyday wellbeing. Speak to your GP, pharmacist or NHS 111 about any health concern, and before changing your diet or exercise a great deal. If you have or have had an eating disorder, the food targets may not be right for you; you can hide numbers in Settings.
- **It is money guidance, not financial advice.** We are not authorised by the Financial Conduct Authority, and Life doesn't give regulated advice about investments, pensions, mortgages, insurance or debt. Tax and benefit figures are estimates from the rules for the [2026/27] tax year and what you told Life. They are not a statement from HMRC. Check anything important with HMRC, an adviser or a free service such as MoneyHelper before you act.
- **It is not legal advice.** Letters and drafts Life writes for you are a starting point. You decide whether to send them.
- **It is not an emergency service.** If you or someone else is in danger, call 999. If you need to talk, Samaritans are free on 116 123, any time.
