# Life support

Email [support@domain] and a person will reply within one working day. If you're writing about your data, [privacy@domain] reaches the same people.

**If you need help now:** in an emergency call 999. To talk to someone, Samaritans are free on 116 123, day or night, or text SHOUT to 85258. For a health question that isn't an emergency, call NHS 111.

## Getting started

### What is Life?

A planner for your day that keeps your health, money and life admin in one place. It works out one clear move at a time, files what you tell it, and fits your plan around how you slept and when you're busy.

### Do I need an account?

No. You can keep everything on your phone. Signing in with Apple, Google or your email backs your life up, so it survives a new phone and reaches your other phone.

### My sign-in code hasn't arrived

Codes arrive within a minute. Check your spam or junk folder, and check the address on the code screen. A code works for 10 minutes; tap "Send a new code" for another. If nothing arrives, use Sign in with Apple or Google instead, or email us.

### Who is Life for?

Adults in the UK. Life asks for your date of birth when you first open it.

## Health

### How do I connect Apple Health?

In Life, go to Plan, Body, Sources and choose Apple Health. Life asks which kinds you want it to read, then iPhone shows its own permission sheet. To change it later, open the Health app on your iPhone, tap your picture, then Apps, then Life.

### How do I connect Health Connect on Android?

In Life, go to Plan, Body, Sources and choose Health Connect. To change it later, open Settings on your phone, then Health Connect, then App permissions, then Life.

### How do I connect WHOOP or Oura?

In Life, go to Plan, Body, Sources and choose WHOOP or Oura. You sign in on their website and choose what to share. Disconnect at any time from the same place.

### Is Life a medical app?

No. Life gives wellness guidance from your own readings. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything. Speak to your GP, a pharmacist or NHS 111 about any health concern.

### Where does my health data go?

Readings from Apple Health, Health Connect and anything you wear stay on your phone. If you sign in, your body numbers and meals are backed up, encrypted, in the UK. The [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md) has the detail.

## Money

### Is this financial advice?

No. Life's money figures are guidance based on published rules and what you tell it. It isn't regulated financial advice. For free, impartial help, try MoneyHelper.

### Does Life connect to my bank?

Not yet. You add the balances, bills and subscriptions you want Life to know about.

## Your data

### How do I see or download what Life holds?

You, Settings, Your data. "See it all" shows every record, and "Download everything" gives you one file.

### How do I delete my account?

You, Settings, Your data, Delete my account. Your account closes straight away. If you sign in again within 30 days it reopens; after that everything is deleted for good. You can also [delete your account on the web](delete.html).

### Does Life use my data to train AI?

No. Life never sells your data, never uses it for advertising and never uses it to train AI models.

### How much does Life cost?

### How do I cancel?

On iPhone: open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Life. On Android: open Google Play, tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions, then Life. Deleting the app doesn't cancel the subscription.

### How do I get a refund?

Apple and Google handle refunds. For Apple, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. For Google Play, open Google Play's refund request page from your order history. If you're stuck, email us and we'll help.

### I bought Life on another phone

Open You, Settings, Life Plus and tap "Restore purchases", signed in to the same Apple or Google account you bought with.
