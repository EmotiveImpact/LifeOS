> Life brings your health, money and life admin into one calm plan for the day.

> MONEY, SORTED
> Bills, subscriptions, renewals and tax estimates in one place, with deadlines caught before they cost you. Guidance from published rules, not financial advice.
>
> FOOD THAT FITS
> Snap a meal or scan a barcode, check the portions, and see how it fits your day. Targets have safe floors and you can hide the numbers.
>
> LEARN IN FIVE MINUTES
> Bite-sized lessons on the things nobody teaches you, like tax codes and pensions, with a quick check at the end.
>
> AN AIM, AND THE STEPS TO IT
