lifeDesign language v2

A simple app that grows with you. It opens as a clean conversation, asks for things with instruments instead of forms, and unlocks new tools as you level up.

How it grows

These are the four levels already in the engine (computeLifeLevelState). Each level adds a little to the app, and nothing appears before it's useful. Tap a level and the Today screen below changes to match.

Seven screens, all live
