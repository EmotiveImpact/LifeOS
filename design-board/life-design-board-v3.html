lifeDesign language v3

Home is where you live your plan. Chat is how life gets you moving and how you figure things out. Everything said gets filed into its place, so life can guide you to the next vantage point.

Rules for the builder

Home is the core

The app opens on Today. Talk and Chat are full-screen layers opened from the bar's mic or text field, and they always close back to Home.

Nothing said is lost

Every recognised fact becomes a typed record (fact, insight, mission, decision, goal) with its source and time, shown on the Understood screen and in Things to know.

Never file silently

Each talk ends with "Here's what I understood", and every item can be removed. Insights always show where they came from.

One question at a time

life asks for at most one thing on Home at a time, using an instrument (choice chips, count stepper, pulse field), never a form.

Nudges earn their place

At most 1 a day. Each one has a reason, a time cost and a value ("6 min · £840"). Snooze choices train when to ask next.

The climb is always visible

Home shows the level pill and "To move up". The vantage screen shows the next peak and what it unlocks. Each level-up plays the cleared sound once.
