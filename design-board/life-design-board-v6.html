v6 · Images at the threshold moments

Beautiful where it counts. Quiet everywhere else.

Photography appears only at the moments that should feel like an occasion: arriving, choosing where you're headed, learning something, levelling up, joining. Everywhere you actually work, the app stays clean. One art direction, one grade, one image per screen at most.

01 · Where images go

Four treatments. Nothing else.

Cover

Full-bleed photo over the top ~70%, fading into the background. Big type sits in the image's calm area.

Welcome · life+ · Level up

Hero band

A photo across the top ~44% of a screen, behind the title only. Content starts on plain background below it.

Goal mission · Lens page · Vantage point

Card image

A photo inside one card, with text on a dark bottom scrim. The card stands out on an otherwise image-free screen.

Today's idea · Learn · Goal choice

Wallpaper

Only outside the app's own screens, like the lock screen behind a nudge or the share card of a weekly recap.

Nudge on lock screen · Recap share card
✕Never on working screens: Today's lists, Plan, tools, money, settings, forms.
✕Never behind chat or Talk. Words need a clean page.
✕Never more than one image per screen, and never two in a row while scrolling.
✕No faces, no stock clichés. Small figures, places, light and objects, so anyone can see themselves.
✕Never text on a busy area. Text sits on the calm part or on the scrim.
✕Never colour-grade by hand. Every image gets the same grade (§ Grade below).
02 · Do / don't

The same screen, two ways.

An image behind a working screen fights every line of text. The same image as one card makes the moment special and leaves the work readable.

03 · The image set

Generated for life: quiet, cinematic 35mm, a single warm ember light against cool shadow, subject small in frame, calm top for text, dark base that fades into the app. No faces, no text.

05 · Grade

How every image is treated.

sourceCommissioned or generated to the art direction above. Portrait 2:3, at least 1170 × 1755 for @3x. WebP or AVIF in the app bundle, under 250 KB each.
gradeSaturation 85%, contrast 102%, plus film grain at 12% (overlay). The same values on every image, applied in code, not baked into the file.
scrimTop: 45% black fading to 0 by 22% (keeps the status bar readable). Bottom: fades to the background colour by 100%. Text on an image must reach 4.5:1 against the scrim.
type on imagePaper white (#F7F8F8) with 72% and 50% for the second and quiet tones. Buttons go paper (primary) or glass (secondary). Never ember text on a photo.
motionA slow drift, scaling 1.00 → 1.06 over 24s, alternating. Off when Reduce Motion is on. Images fade in over 400ms once loaded, over a light-field fallback.
light modeImages keep their dark grade. Only the bottom fade changes, ending in the light background. The status bar flips to light content over covers and bands.
fallbackIf an image hasn't loaded or fails, the screen shows its domain light field (v4 §27). The layout never depends on the photo.
expoexpo-image with contentFit="cover", transition={400}, a blurhash placeholder, and expo-linear-gradient for scrims. One <ImageTreatment kind="cover|band|card"> component owns all of this.
