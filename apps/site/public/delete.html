Delete your Life account

You can delete your account in the app: You, Settings, Your data, Delete my account. If you don't have the app any more, you can do it here.

What happens

  • Your account closes straight away and you're signed out on every phone.
  • If you sign in again within 30 days, your account reopens as it was.
  • After 30 days, everything Life's servers hold for you is deleted: your profile, body numbers, meals, records, plans, people, memories, settings, kept letters and photos, connected wearables and sign-in details. Database backups roll off within [7] days after that.
  • Nothing is kept, except the anonymous weekly tally, which never held anything about you.

This doesn't cancel a subscription: cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings. It also doesn't touch what's on your phone: delete the app to remove that.

Delete it now

Or email [privacy@domain] from the address you sign in with, and we'll delete it within [one working day] and confirm by email. If you signed in with Apple and hid your email, write from the address your Apple ID forwards to and tell us so.
